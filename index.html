Welcome to the
Crazy Cat Lady's Cat Sanctuary

Pull up a porch chair, dearie. The cats are coming, up to ten at a time! Give each arrival a Welcome Vet Check and a name, nurse poorly kitties back to health in the clinic kennels, and find happy cats their forever homes. Adoption fees buy cat food and medicine; my garden feeds me (and grows catnip!).
